Frequently asked questions

Why record a memorial or milestone on Bitcoin?

We share life's important moments through photographs, social media, and memorial websites. But websites can disappear, accounts can be closed, and digital memories can be lost. Continuum offers another way to preserve what matters. For $21, your personal message is recorded directly on Bitcoin's blockchain—not simply stored on a website or represented by a cryptographic hash. Your words become part of a durable, independently verifiable historical record that doesn't depend on Continuum or any other company to remain accessible.

Share your memories anywhere. Preserve your words on Bitcoin.

What is actually preserved on Bitcoin?

The approved short inscription is included in a Bitcoin transaction. The transaction can be independently verified using a public Bitcoin explorer. The Continuum webpage, images, and extended descriptions are hosted separately and are not themselves guaranteed to remain available forever.

Do I need a Bitcoin wallet?

No. You can request a memorial or milestone without a wallet or any prior Bitcoin experience.

Can I change my message later?

Once the transaction is confirmed, the on-chain message cannot be edited. That's why we ask for your approval first. Separately hosted presentation details may be updated.

Can I add a photo or longer story?

Ask us about adding supporting details to your viewer page. Those additions are hosted separately from the on-chain inscription.

When do I pay?

After you approve your memorial or milestone preview, we'll provide payment instructions. Once payment is received, we'll create and submit your inscription to Bitcoin. When the transaction is confirmed, we'll send you a link to view and independently verify your inscription.
